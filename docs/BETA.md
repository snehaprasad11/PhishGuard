# Trying PhishGuard (beta)

Thank you for helping test PhishGuard. It takes about five minutes to set up.

**What it is:** a browser extension that gives you a second opinion on a suspicious link or
message. **What it is not:** a guarantee. It will miss some phishing and will sometimes be wrong
about a legitimate site.

**Privacy:** PhishGuard never scans anything until you click Analyse, and nothing leaves your
device. There is no account and no server.

## Install

1. Download `phishguard-extension-0.3.0.zip` from the
   [latest release](https://github.com/snehaprasad11/PhishGuard/releases/latest) and unzip it
   somewhere you will not delete it, such as your Documents folder.
2. Open Chrome or Edge and go to `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions`).
3. Turn on **Developer mode**.
4. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the unzipped folder (the one containing `manifest.json`).
5. Click the puzzle-piece button in the toolbar and pin **PhishGuard**.

Your browser may show a reminder that a developer-mode extension is installed. That is expected
for a beta that is not yet in the store.

## Try it

- On any website, click the shield icon and then **Analyse this page**.
- When you get a suspicious text or email, copy it, paste it into the box, and click **Analyse
  pasted text**. You do not need to open the link.
- If a result is wrong, click **This result is wrong**. That is stored on your device only.

## What I would love to hear

1. Did the verdict match your gut feeling?
2. Was anything flagged that you know is safe? Which site or what kind of message?
3. Did it miss something you are sure was a scam?
4. Were the explanations understandable? Was anything confusing?
5. Would you use it again? If not, why not?

Please do not send me anything private. For a wrongly flagged site, the address without
anything after a `?` is enough; for a message, a description of what kind it was is enough.

## Remove it

Go to `chrome://extensions`, find PhishGuard and click **Remove**. This also deletes its
history and reports.
